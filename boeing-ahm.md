# Boeing – Airplane Health Management (AHM)

## Overview
Led product strategy and execution for Boeing’s Airplane Health Management (AHM) platform, enabling real-time monitoring and predictive maintenance across global airline fleets.

## Problem
- Limited real-time visibility into aircraft health  
- Reactive and inefficient maintenance workflows  
- Data fragmentation across systems  
- High operational and maintenance costs  

## What I Built
- Developed an IoT-enabled platform integrating aircraft sensor data  
- Enabled predictive maintenance through real-time analytics  
- Delivered mobile applications supporting operational workflows  
- Defined platform roadmap across embedded systems and digital interfaces  

## Impact
- Generated $15M+ ARR across 50+ airline customers  
- Improved operational efficiency and reduced downtime  
- Increased adoption through enhanced visibility and usability  
- Scaled globally across enterprise aviation environments  

## Why It Matters
Demonstrates:
- IoT and real-time data platform experience  
- Distributed systems and edge computing  
- Large-scale enterprise product delivery  
- Strong business impact and adoption
