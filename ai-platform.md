# AI Platform – Agentic Workflows & Developer Tooling

## Overview
Designed and developed an AI platform leveraging modern agentic workflows to support AI-assisted product development and automation.

## Problem
- AI workflows were fragmented and difficult to operationalize  
- Developers lacked structured tools for building multi-step AI systems  
- Integration between data retrieval, reasoning, and execution was inefficient  

## What I Built
- Architected AI workflows using:
  - Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG)  
  - Function calling  
  - Multi-step reasoning patterns  
- Designed end-to-end system architecture:
  - Data retrieval pipelines  
  - Orchestration layers  
  - Developer-facing tooling  
- Defined scalable platform architecture for AI-assisted workflows  

## Impact
- Enabled structured development of AI-powered features  
- Improved efficiency of multi-step reasoning workflows  
- Demonstrated practical application of agentic AI systems  

## Why It Matters
Demonstrates:
- Hands-on experience with modern AI systems  
- Understanding of LLM-based architectures  
- Ability to translate emerging technologies into product platforms  
- Strong foundation for AI and data platform roles  

## Notes
This project represents independent work and experimentation with emerging AI paradigms and system design patterns.
