# PayPal – Global Payouts Platform (Hyperwallet)

## Overview
Led product delivery and platform modernization for PayPal’s global payouts system (Hyperwallet), supporting millions of users and complex B2B2C payment workflows.

## Problem
- Legacy payout systems lacked scalability and flexibility  
- No unified mobile-first experience  
- Partner integrations were slow and inconsistent  
- Limited extensibility for new business models  

## What I Built
- Delivered a mobile payouts platform (iOS & Android) in 6 months  
- Designed a white-label platform architecture enabling partner customization  
- Defined API contracts and integration patterns for scalable partner onboarding  
- Standardized platform workflows across engineering, product, and operations  

## Impact
- Successfully delivered a previously stalled program (3+ years)  
- Enabled new B2B2C partner-driven business models  
- Improved platform extensibility and reduced integration friction  
- Accelerated time-to-market for new payout use cases  

## Why It Matters
Demonstrates strong experience in:
- Platform product design  
- API-driven ecosystems  
- Large-scale financial systems  
- Execution in complex, high-stakes environments
